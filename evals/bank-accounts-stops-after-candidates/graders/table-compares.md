The reply compares the candidates in a table, with a column for each
candidate and a row for each requirement, or group of requirements, that it
compares. In each row except the row for Other processes, each cell starts with
one of these phrases: "inside one stream", "across streams: a workflow of N
steps", "a read model", "the app only", "cannot keep it" or "does not arise".
A cell can add a few words after its phrase.
