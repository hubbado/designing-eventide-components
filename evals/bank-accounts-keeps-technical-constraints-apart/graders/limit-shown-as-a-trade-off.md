The reply shows the reviewer that the 30-day limit on reversals comes from
the platform team's rule on lists that grow, and not from a need of the
business. It names the limit as a trade-off between that rule and the
reversal of a deposit, or it asks the reviewer whether the business needs the
limit. A reply that gives the 30-day limit as a requirement, with no word
that the rule causes it, fails.
