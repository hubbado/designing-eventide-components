The reply compares the candidates in a table. One of its columns is the
current structure, reversals handled on the account's own stream, in its best
version: with no list of deposits and none of the fixes to that list, for
example with each old deposit found by a read of the account's own stream. A
table whose only column for reversals on the account's stream is the account
with its lists and their fixes fails.
